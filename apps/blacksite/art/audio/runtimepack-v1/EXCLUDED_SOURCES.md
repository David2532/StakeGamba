# Excluded Sources

The following popular “free” libraries were deliberately not placed in this redistributable ZIP.

## Mixkit

Excluded even though it was the initial reference site. The current terms restrict aggregating/redistributing raw items as a stock library, and the Envato Acceptable Use Policy applying to Mixkit prohibits content promoting weapons or gambling. The stock-music license also does not cover video games.

- <https://mixkit.co/license/#sfxFree>
- <https://mixkit.co/terms/>
- <https://help.elements.envato.com/hc/en-us/articles/31035788503321-Acceptable-Use-Policy>

## Pixabay

Excluded because the current content license prohibits standalone distribution of audio. “Free download” is not permission to republish a raw sound library.

- <https://pixabay.com/service/terms/>

## Sonniss GDC bundles

Excluded because use in a finished game is different from redistributing the raw sounds as a library.

- <https://sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc/>
- <https://sonniss.com/gdc-bundle-license/>

## Attribution or unclear-license candidates

The separate OpenGameArt “Penguin SFX” pack licensed CC BY 3.0 was excluded. This archive uses the different **Penguin Sounds** CC0 source instead. Freesound previews and any asset with unclear commercial/gambling or raw-redistribution rights were also excluded.

This document records conservative curation decisions and is not legal advice.
