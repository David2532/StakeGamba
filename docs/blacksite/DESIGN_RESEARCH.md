## Scope and method

This review covers direct slot references, adjacent high-quality product interfaces, current design systems and the official approval/accessibility/performance constraints that materially affect BLACKSITE. Actual public pages were opened on the research date rather than relying on search-result thumbnails. Provider game detail pages and public product/design documentation were inspected from their live published content. The Stake Engine documentation URLs returned their current client-rendered loading shell to text extraction, so the observations below were cross-checked against the repository's dated official-source review; they must be reviewed again in a fully rendered browser immediately before submission. Legal-age interstitials were not bypassed and no authenticated, real-money or region-gated game session was launched, so no undocumented in-game interaction is claimed.

Mobile conclusions come from the providers' published responsive requirements and the repository's independently authored portrait contract, not from an unperformed real-device inspection of competitor games. No third-party asset, copy, source code or layout composition was imported.

## Synthesized visual identity

BLACKSITE is an **operational vault-breach console**, not a generic neon casino skin. Its identity is built from physical armored-vault mass, a precise square grid, restrained telemetry and the adult-toned Penguin Vaultkeeper. The interface uses:

- blue-black and graphite as quiet structural surfaces;
- desaturated cyan for system connectivity and passive state;
- amber for focus, caution and player attention;
- controlled breach red for selection, escalation and failure;
- a wide industrial sans hierarchy for product/state headlines, paired with a local monospace telemetry voice;
- deep spacing and material layers on desktop, but a board-first vertical stack and deliberate character omission on portrait/low-height layouts;
- semantic, result-driven motion that always resolves, shortens in Turbo, disappears under Reduced Motion and never determines payout.

The board, WIN amount and legal action remain the first three priorities. Character, atmosphere and background motion are subordinate. The symbol family must replace Unicode/greybox representations with six original, equally bounded, shape-distinct marks; hue alone may not encode identity. The pending inline-vector candidate follows that direction but remains exact-package and human-review pending. Final title/brand art, board art, supporting UI art, gameplay FX, BLACKOUT/foreground layers, approved Spine 4.2 rig/clips, authored audio and game-tile package remain governed by `M3_ASSET_MANIFEST.md` and cannot be declared complete from this research.

## Explicit non-adoptions

- No competitor composition, mechanic, wording, symbol, mascot treatment or downloadable marketing asset is copied.
- No full-screen persistent glow, dense particle field or cinematic may reduce board, cost, payout or control legibility.
- No desktop side character is retained on compact layouts merely for visual parity.
- No colour-only selected/error state, hover-only explanation or pointer-only critical action is accepted.
- No lab timing, synthetic screenshot or provider marketing claim is presented as real-user field evidence.

## Research stop condition

Broad research stopped after the sources above converged on the same material decisions: authored identity, state-led hierarchy, explicit mode communication, independently composed portrait layout, bounded/reduced motion, accessible modal focus and performance-aware media. Additional references reviewed at this point did not produce a new product decision and were therefore not added as a link list.
