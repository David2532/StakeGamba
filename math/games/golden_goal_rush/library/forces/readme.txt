force_record_base.json / force_record_bonus.json are generated by `run.py publish`
(see optimization.py:build_force_records). They catalogue representative,
already-generated, unmodified books per outcome category (no_win,
max_win_observed, typical_win, scatter_trigger) for QA and hit-rate bookkeeping.

The MVP generator does not implement reel forcing (game_override.py raises
NotImplementedError), so these are indexed natural examples, not synthesized
or forced outcomes.
